2. What TravelBuddy is

TravelBuddy is a marketplace that connects senders who need a parcel delivered with travelers already heading toward the same destination. We provide the matching, tracking, payment, and safety tools. TravelBuddy is not a courier or logistics company, and travelers act as independent individuals, not employees or agents of TravelBuddy.

3. Eligibility & account responsibilities

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.
  • You must provide accurate identity and contact information, and keep it up to date.
  • You are responsible for all activity that happens under your account, so keep your login credentials secure.
  • Travelers may be required to complete identity verification before accepting parcel requests.

4. Sending a parcel

  • Senders must accurately describe the parcel's contents, size, and weight when posting a request.
  • Senders may not post parcels containing items listed on our Prohibited Items page.
  • Once a traveler accepts a request, the sender agrees to have the parcel ready for the agreed pickup window.
  • Payment is held securely by TravelBuddy and released to the traveler only after the receiver confirms delivery using the OTP sent to them.

5. Traveling & delivering

  • Travelers agree to handle parcels with reasonable care and deliver them to the destination stated in the request.
  • Travelers may inspect a parcel before accepting it and may decline any request that appears to violate our policies.
  • Travelers must not open, tamper with, or misuse a parcel's contents.
  • Earnings are calculated per completed, OTP-confirmed delivery and paid out according to the payout schedule shown in your wallet.

6. Payments, fees & cancellations

  • Delivery fees are shown before a sender confirms a request and may vary by distance, parcel size, and urgency.
  • TravelBuddy may retain a service fee from each completed transaction.
  • If a traveler cancels before pickup, the request returns to matching and any payment already held remains protected until a new traveler is found or the sender cancels the request.
  • Refunds are handled case by case through Support when a delivery cannot be completed as agreed.

All users agree to follow our Community Guidelines. TravelBuddy may suspend or remove accounts that engage in fraud, harassment, repeated cancellations, misrepresenting parcel contents, or any activity that endangers other users.

9. Disputes

If something goes wrong with a delivery, contact Support as soon as possible with your order details. We'll review the parcel and trip records, in-app messages, and delivery status to help resolve the issue fairly between the sender and traveler.
