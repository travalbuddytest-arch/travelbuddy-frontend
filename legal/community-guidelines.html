Community Guidelines

Building a safe, trustworthy, and respectful peer-to-peer parcel delivery network for all Senders and Travelers.

Last updated: October 2026
Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Prohibited Items Community Guidelines

At CarryParcel, our platform thrives on mutual trust, clear communication, and personal safety. Senders trust Travelers with their valuable parcels, and Travelers trust Senders to declare item contents accurately. These Community Guidelines set the standard of conduct expected from every platform member.

1. Respectful Communication

Treat every member of the CarryParcel community with dignity and courtesy. In-app communication—whether via chat messages, calls, or pickup interactions—must remain civil, professional, and friendly.

2. Harassment

Harassment in any form is strictly forbidden. This includes sending repeated unwanted messages, using derogatory slurs, intimidating users, or pursuing personal relationships outside the context of parcel logistics.

3. Threats and Intimidation

Making physical, financial, or verbal threats against another user, their property, or CarryParcel staff will result in immediate permanent account termination and potential police escalation.

4. Hate and Abusive Conduct

We enforce zero tolerance for hate speech, discrimination, or abusive conduct targeting individuals based on race, religion, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

5. Fraud

Engaging in dishonest practices to extract unearned funds, claim false parcel damages, or circumvent platform fees violates core community standards.

6. Scams

Attempting to trick community members into transferring funds outside the CarryParcel escrow system, providing advance payments off-platform, or sharing banking credentials is strictly forbidden.

7. Impersonation

Users must not impersonate other individuals, CarryParcel support personnel, law enforcement officers, or corporate representatives.

8. Fake Accounts

Creating dummy or duplicate accounts to artificially manipulate user ratings, bypass account bans, or orchestrate fraudulent parcel requests is prohibited.

9. Spam

Sending unsolicited commercial advertisements, repetitive unwanted messages, or promotional links through CarryParcel chat or support channels is prohibited.

10. Misleading Information

Users must provide genuine information. Falsifying travel schedules, destination locations, or vehicle types misleads community members and disrupts logistics coordination.

11. False Parcel Declarations

Intentionally understating parcel weight, hiding hazardous items, or misrepresenting parcel contents undermines traveler safety and constitutes a severe breach of community trust.

12. Privacy Violations

Respect the personal privacy of fellow users. Photographing users without consent, recording pickup interactions inappropriately, or misusing personal contact information received for delivery coordination is forbidden.

13. Sharing Personal Information

Do not publicly post, share, or broadcast another user's private details, phone numbers, home addresses, or identity documents ("doxxing").

14. Chat Misuse

In-app chat tools are intended exclusively for delivery coordination, schedule confirmation, and location agreements. Misusing chat for spam, off-platform solicitations, or inappropriate conversations is prohibited.

15. Calling Misuse

In-app calling features should be used respectfully and solely for delivery coordination during active trips. Repeated late-night calling or harassing phone calls will result in feature restriction.

16. Unsafe Behavior

Always prioritize safety. Meet in public, well-lit areas for parcel handoffs whenever possible. Never drive recklessly while carrying parcels or demand that a Traveler break transit regulations.

17. Illegal Activity

Utilizing CarryParcel to facilitate illegal transactions, transport stolen property, or evade regulatory authorities is strictly forbidden.

18. Manipulation

Manipulating the review system through fake reviews, rating extortion ("give me a discount or I'll leave 1 star"), or collusion is prohibited.

19. Platform Abuse

Exploiting software bugs, attempting unauthorized API requests, or disrupting CarryParcel system infrastructure damages the platform for everyone.

20. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness a violation of these Community Guidelines, please report it immediately through the in-app reporting tools or contact our support team confidentially.

21. Enforcement

CarryParcel investigates reported violations thoroughly and impartially. Depending on severity, enforcement actions include formal warnings, temporary feature limits, account suspension, or permanent ban and law enforcement escalation.
