How TravelBuddy Works

TravelBuddy connects two sides of the same trip: people who need to send a parcel, and travelers already heading that way. Here's exactly how a delivery moves from post to drop-off.

Send a Parcel Pickup a Parcel

For Senders: Sending a Parcel

Post your parcel

Add pickup point, destination, size and category.

Get matched

A verified traveler on your route accepts your request.

OTP pickup

Hand over your parcel using a one-time pickup code.

Track & confirm

Watch delivery progress and confirm with the delivery OTP.

For Travelers: Picking up a Parcel

Post your trip

Share your route and travel dates.

Accept parcels

Choose parcel requests that match your journey.

Pick up with OTP

Confirm pickup from the sender securely.

Deliver & earn

Complete delivery with the drop-off OTP and get paid.

What Keeps It Safe

Every traveler is verified before they can accept parcels. Both pickup and delivery require a one-time password, so a parcel only changes hands with the right person at each step. Payment is held securely and released once delivery is confirmed, and TravelBuddy's support team is available for disputes. Full details are on our Safety page.
