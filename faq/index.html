Frequently Asked Questions

What is TravelBuddy?

TravelBuddy is a community-based parcel delivery platform that connects people who need to send a parcel with verified travelers already heading the same way.

How do I post a parcel?

Register or log in, then open Post a Parcel from your dashboard and enter your pickup point, destination, and parcel details.

How do I become a traveler and carry parcels?

Post your trip route and travel dates from your dashboard, then accept parcel requests that match your journey.

How much does it cost to send a parcel?

Pricing depends on your route, parcel size and category. You'll see the price before you confirm a match with a traveler.

Is TravelBuddy safe to use?

Travelers are verified before they can accept parcels, and both pickup and delivery require an OTP confirmation. Payment is held until delivery is confirmed.

What is the pickup and delivery OTP for?

The OTP confirms that a parcel is handed to and received by the correct person, at both pickup and delivery.

Can I track my parcel?

Yes, once a traveler accepts your parcel you can follow its status from your TravelBuddy dashboard through to delivery.

What items can't be sent through TravelBuddy?

Certain items are restricted for safety and legal reasons. See our Prohibited Items policy for the full list.

What happens if there's a problem with a delivery?

TravelBuddy's support team can help resolve disputes — reach out through the Support Center or Contact page.

Is posting a parcel free?

Yes, posting a parcel is free. You only pay once a traveler accepts and the delivery is arranged.

Still have questions?

Our support team is happy to help with anything not covered here.
