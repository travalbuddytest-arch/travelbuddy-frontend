A Different Kind of Parcel Delivery

Most parcel delivery services rely on depots, hubs and scheduled runs. TravelBuddy instead matches your parcel with a verified traveler who is already going where it needs to go — cutting out the hub-to-hub handling and keeping delivery affordable.

What Makes TravelBuddy's Delivery Service Work

Community parcel delivery

Real travelers picking up real parcels along routes they're already taking.

Affordable parcel delivery

Pricing reflects the route, not a nationwide courier network's overhead.

Parcel delivery between cities

Wherever TravelBuddy travelers are heading, sends can follow.

Live tracking

Follow your parcel's status from acceptance to delivery.

For Senders and Travelers

If you need something delivered, post a parcel and let a matching traveler pickup it. If you're already travelling, pickup a parcel along your route and earn for the trip you're already making. Both sides go through the same safety framework — verification, OTP handovers, and protected payments.
