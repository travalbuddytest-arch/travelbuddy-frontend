How It Works

Posting a parcel on CarryParcel is designed to be clear, structured, and simple. Follow these steps to submit a parcel request and connect with an available traveler.

01

Create Your Parcel Request

Access the parcel creation form on your user dashboard to start your delivery listing.

02

Add Pickup Details

Provide accurate pickup coordinates, contact numbers, and preferred handover times.

03

Add Destination

Specify the drop-off location, recipient contact details, and destination timing expectations.

04

Add Parcel Information

Detail item category, dimensions, weight category, package photos, and fragile handling notes.

05

Submit Your Request

Publish your parcel listing securely to match with active route travelers moving in your direction.

06

Connect and Coordinate

Review requests from community travelers, check user ratings and KYC verification status, and accept a match.

07

Manage Your Delivery

Track status updates from pickup to final recipient QR verification code check.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post a parcel on CarryParcel?

Log in to your account, click on "Post a Parcel", fill out pickup, drop-off, and item details, and publish your listing to active route travelers.

What details are required to post a package?

You need pickup address, drop-off location, recipient phone number, package size/weight category, and item description.

How do I know my parcel is in good hands?

Travelers complete identity verification, and you can review user ratings and delivery histories before accepting a match.
