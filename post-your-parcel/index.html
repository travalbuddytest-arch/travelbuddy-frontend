How It Works

Posting a parcel on TravelBuddy is designed to be simple, fast, and transparent. We remove the complex logistics of traditional couriers and connect you directly with people already travelling your route.

1. Enter Details

Provide pickup and destination points, parcel size, and category.

2. Set Your Price

Choose a fair delivery fee for the traveler.

3. Get Matched

Verified travelers heading your way will browse and accept your request.

4. Safe Delivery

Track your parcel and confirm delivery with a secure OTP.

Why Post on TravelBuddy?

Security
