The Delivery Journey

Follow the direct pathway of a parcel on CarryParcel from initial listing creation to final recipient handover.

01

Parcel Posted

Sender creates a detailed delivery listing on CarryParcel specifying pickup and destination locations.

02

Connection Matched

Verified traveler heading along the route reviews and accepts the parcel delivery request.

03

Pickup Handover

Traveler meets the sender at the designated location and receives the packaged parcel.

04

In Transit

Package moves directly along the traveler's planned journey route toward the destination.

05

Delivery Confirmation

Recipient inspects item and completes delivery confirmation via secure QR code scan.
