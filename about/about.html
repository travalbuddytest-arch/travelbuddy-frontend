About CarryParcel

CarryParcel is a community-powered parcel delivery platform that connects people who need to send parcels with travelers who are already traveling toward the same destination.

Our story

Our Story

CarryParcel connects parcel requirements with existing journeys and provides a structured digital experience for senders and travelers.

Our goal is to make it easier for people to coordinate parcel delivery around journeys that are already happening.

  • A structured experience for parcel senders and travelers
  • Parcel and journey details managed in one place
  • A community-powered approach to delivery
How CarryParcel Works

Built around shared journeys

For Senders

Post parcel requirements, provide pickup and destination details, discover suitable travelers, and manage your delivery journey.

For Travelers

Share upcoming journeys, discover parcel requests that match your routes, and choose parcels you are willing to carry.

Our Mission

Make parcel delivery more connected to the journeys people are already taking.

Our Vision

Build a clear, useful digital network for people sending and carrying parcels.
