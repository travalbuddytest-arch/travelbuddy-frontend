Our story

Why we built TravelBuddy

If someone in Pune needs to get a parcel to Delhi, and a registered traveler is already driving or flying that route, the two can connect directly on TravelBuddy. They agree on a fair delivery fee, the traveler carries the parcel safely, and the recipient — or a trusted friend on their behalf — collects it at the destination.

By removing traditional courier middlemen, intercity delivery becomes personal, cost-effective, and eco-friendly. Travelers earn extra income along a journey they were already making, while senders get same-day or next-day delivery without high courier charges.

  • No middlemen, no inflated courier fees
  • Every traveler ID-verified before matching
  • OTP-confirmed handover on every delivery
