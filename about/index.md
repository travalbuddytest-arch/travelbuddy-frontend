# About CarryParcel

> Connecting people and parcels through shared journeys. Faster, affordable, community-powered delivery.

## What CarryParcel Does

CarryParcel is a community-powered parcel delivery platform built in Pune, Maharashtra, India. It connects people who need to send a parcel with travelers who already have an upcoming trip heading toward the same destination, so parcels ride journeys that are being made anyway instead of triggering a separate courier run.

## How It Works

1. **Post a requirement** — add pickup and destination details, and declare weight, size and category.
2. **Review matches** — verified travelers already heading your way are surfaced, with their trip history and ratings visible.
3. **Confirm and pay** — payment is held when you confirm a match and released to the traveler only after the receiver confirms delivery.
4. **Track to the door** — follow live status through handover; the receiver confirms with a secure QR code at the moment of delivery.

## For Senders and Travelers

- **Senders** post parcel requirements, compare matched travelers, and manage the delivery from their dashboard.
- **Travelers** add upcoming trips, browse parcel requests that match their routes, choose what to carry, and withdraw earnings to their bank.
- Every account can do both. There is no separate sender or traveler account type.

## Key Principles

- **Community-Powered**: Direct person-to-person collaboration on journeys already in progress.
- **Safety First**: Senders and travelers complete ID verification before matching.
- **Payment Protection**: Funds are held and only released on confirmed delivery.
- **Prohibited Items**: Items that cannot legally or safely travel are listed and enforced.

## Our Mission

Make parcel delivery fit the journey that already exists.

## Our Vision

A network people can rely on for moving parcels between cities without a courier.
