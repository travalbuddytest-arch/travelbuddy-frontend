# Contact CarryParcel

> Reach the CarryParcel team for support, parcel delivery questions, or developer enquiries.

## Contact Information

- **Support Email**: `support@carryparcel.in`
- **Help Center**: https://www.carryparcel.in/support/support.html
- **Website**: https://www.carryparcel.in/
- **Location**: Pune, Maharashtra, India

## What to Contact Us About

- **Sending a parcel** — posting a requirement, declaring weight/size/category, or understanding a match.
- **Carrying a parcel on a trip** — adding a journey, choosing requests, and withdrawals.
- **Payments & wallet** — payment holds, releases, and transaction history.
- **Verification** — ID verification status for senders and travelers.
- **Developer / API** — the public API and developer portal at https://www.carryparcel.in/developers/index.html

## Raising a Support Ticket

Sign in and open the Support Center in your dashboard. Tickets can be created and followed there, and your full ticket history stays in one place. A public contact form is also available at https://www.carryparcel.in/contact, which submits to the CarryParcel backend.

## Safety Note

CarryParcel will never ask for your password or your Delivery QR Code over email or phone. Anyone who does is not CarryParcel.
