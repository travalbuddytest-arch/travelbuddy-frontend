Post a Parcel How It Works

What does "Post a Parcel" mean on TravelBuddy?

TravelBuddy is a community-based parcel delivery platform. Instead of handing your parcel to a courier network of warehouses and depots, you post the details of what needs to move — and a traveler who is already heading in that direction picks it up along their route. Posting a parcel is the first step: it puts your delivery request in front of travelers who can pick it up.

How to Post a Parcel

  1. Create a TravelBuddy account or log in.
  2. Open Post a Parcel from your dashboard.
  3. Enter pickup location, destination, parcel size/category and preferred timing.
  4. Review and confirm — your post becomes visible to matching travelers.
  5. Once a traveler accepts, coordinate pickup using the in-app OTP.

Posting itself takes only a couple of minutes. The actual parcel-posting form lives inside your TravelBuddy account — log in or register to get started.

Safety and Verification

Every traveler on TravelBuddy goes through identity verification before they can accept parcels. Pickup and delivery are both locked behind a one-time password (OTP), so a parcel can only be handed over to the right person at the right step. Payments are held securely and released once delivery is confirmed, and our support team can step in if a dispute comes up. Read more on our Safety page.

Pickup and Delivery Tracking

Once a traveler accepts your parcel, you'll be able to follow its status — accepted, picked up, in transit, and delivered — from your TravelBuddy dashboard, right up to the OTP-confirmed handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post a parcel on TravelBuddy?

Register or log in, describe your parcel's size, pickup point and destination, and TravelBuddy matches you with a verified traveler already heading that way.

Is posting a parcel free?

Creating a parcel post is free. You only pay once a traveler accepts your parcel, and the price is shown upfront before you confirm.

How is my parcel kept safe?

Travelers are verified, pickup and delivery are both confirmed with an OTP, and payment is held securely until the delivery is confirmed.

Can I track my parcel after it's posted?

Yes. Once a traveler accepts your parcel, you can follow its status from pickup through to delivery on your TravelBuddy dashboard.
