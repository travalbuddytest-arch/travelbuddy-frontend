Safety & Parcel Protection on TravelBuddy

Every delivery on TravelBuddy runs through the same set of protections — for the person sending a parcel and the traveler carrying it.

Post a Parcel Read FAQ

Traveler Verification

Before a traveler can accept parcels on TravelBuddy, they go through an identity verification step. This gives senders confidence about who is picking up and carrying their parcel.

OTP-Protected Pickup and Delivery

Pickup OTP

The sender shares a one-time code that the traveler confirms at pickup, so the parcel only leaves with the right person.

Delivery OTP

A separate one-time code confirms the parcel reaches the correct recipient at the destination.

Payment Protection

Payment for a delivery is held securely through TravelBuddy and only released to the traveler once the delivery OTP confirms the parcel has arrived. This protects senders from paying for a delivery that never happened, and protects travelers by guaranteeing payment once their side of the delivery is complete.

Parcel Tracking

From the moment a traveler accepts a parcel, senders can follow its status — accepted, picked up, in transit, delivered — from their TravelBuddy dashboard.

Dispute Support

If something doesn't go as expected with a delivery, TravelBuddy's support team is available to help resolve it. You can reach out any time through our Support Center or Contact page.

Parcel Protection Eligibility

Protection applies to parcels posted and accepted through TravelBuddy that follow our accepted categories and sizes. Items on our Prohibited Items list are not eligible for posting or protection. Review our Terms & Conditions for full eligibility details.
