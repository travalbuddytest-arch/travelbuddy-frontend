HOW A DELIVERY WORKS

What every parcel goes through

Verification first

Travelers complete ID verification before they are able to accept parcels, and both pickup and delivery are confirmed by Secure QR Code.

Payment held until delivery

Payment is held when a match is confirmed and released only once the receiver confirms delivery.

Clear parcel rules

What may travel is listed on the prohibited items page, and travelers see the declared contents and category before deciding to accept.

Trips that already run

A parcel rides a journey somebody is already making, so it does not add a separate delivery run for someone else.
