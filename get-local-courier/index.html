How It Works

Getting local delivery support on CarryParcel follows a clear, step-by-step workflow designed for senders and travelers alike.

01

Share Delivery Requirements

Specify item category, package size category, photo, and preferred transport timeframe on your dashboard.

02

Add Pickup Details

Provide accurate pickup location instructions and contact details to assist the traveler with handover.

03

Add Destination

Include complete drop-off details, address landmarks, and recipient verification contact numbers.

04

Find a Suitable Connection

Review active travel participants heading along your route and evaluate user ratings and verification status.

05

Coordinate Delivery

Use built-in in-app messaging tools to agree on precise pickup times and meeting locations.

06

Complete Delivery

Handover is completed when the recipient receives the package and verifies delivery using the secure QR / OTP code.

Why CarryParcel?

Factual, community-centered benefits of using active local route connections.

Direct Transport

Parcels travel directly with community travelers along active routes without stopping at central sorting hubs.

In-App Communication

Senders and travelers communicate in real-time through built-in chat tools to coordinate handover timing.

Identity Verification Checks

Travelers submit government ID details and liveness verification before accepting parcel delivery requests.

Careful Individual Handling

Community travelers transport packages personally rather than piling them in bulk freight containers.

Trust & Safety

All community participants agree to uphold high standards of safety, clear communication, and responsible behavior.

User Identity Verification

KYC checks support user identity verification across the CarryParcel platform prior to route matches.

Responsible Handling

Travelers must treat all items with care, inspect outer packaging at pickup, and never tamper with sealed packages.

Respectful Conduct

In-app communications and meetups must adhere strictly to CarryParcel's Community Guidelines.
