Affordable For Everyone

CarryParcel makes fast parcel transport practical and accessible by utilizing unused travel capacity on active trips, keeping fees fair, transparent, and user-focused.

Post a Parcel Learn Value Concept

A Practical Delivery Option

By connecting senders directly with travelers who are already taking the journey, CarryParcel creates value without the heavy overhead costs of traditional logistics fleets.

Zero Fleet Overhead

Because travelers are already making the trip, marginal travel capacity is put to productive use without vehicle fleet maintenance.

No Warehouse Handling Fees

Packages travel directly from sender to traveler to recipient, bypassing warehouse sorting markups.

Resource Optimization

Utilizing existing travel trips reduces the need for additional dedicated freight runs, making it an eco-friendly option.

No Surprise Fees
