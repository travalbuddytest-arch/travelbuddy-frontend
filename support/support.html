Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers to common questions about using CarryParcel.

Still need help? Email support
How do I post a parcel?

Sign in to your CarryParcel account, open the parcel posting flow, enter the required pickup, destination and parcel details, then review the information before submitting it.

How can I track my parcel?

Sign in and open your dashboard to view the tracking information available for your active parcel.

Where can I check my wallet and transactions?

Open Dashboard → Wallet after signing in to review your available wallet information and transaction history.

How do I complete account verification?

Use the verification section in your account and follow the instructions shown there for submitting the required verification information.

What should I do if I have a technical problem?

Note the page or feature where the problem occurred, keep your parcel or trip ID if relevant, and raise a support ticket or email support@carryparcel.in with those details.

What information should I include when contacting support?

Include the email or account identifier associated with your account, the parcel or trip ID when relevant, what happened, and any useful screenshots. Do not include your password, OTP or delivery verification code.
