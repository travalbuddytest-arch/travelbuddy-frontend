Pickup a Parcel While Travelling

Already heading somewhere? Add your trip to TravelBuddy and pick up parcels from senders along your route. It's a simple way to earn money picking up parcel deliveries you were making the trip for anyway.

Traveler Parcel Delivery, Explained

Traveler-based parcel delivery means real people — not depot-to-depot logistics — pickup parcels along journeys they're already making. As a traveler on TravelBuddy, you post your trip route and dates, senders whose parcels match that route can request you pickup their parcel, and you earn for each delivery you complete.

How Picking up a Parcel Works

Post your trip

Add your travel route and dates to your TravelBuddy profile.

Accept a parcel

Review parcel requests that match your route and accept one.

OTP pickup

Confirm pickup from the sender using the in-app OTP.

Deliver & earn

Hand it over at the destination with a delivery OTP and get paid.

Getting Verified as a Traveler

To keep the community safe, travelers complete identity verification before they can accept parcels. Once verified, you can browse and accept parcel requests that match any trip you post. Learn more about how verification and protection work on our Safety page.
