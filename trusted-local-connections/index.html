Community-Driven Connections

CarryParcel connects senders and travelers through a structured marketplace, providing technical safeguards and clear standards of conduct.

Identity & Verification

KYC Identity Checks

Travelers submit government-issued identification and liveness verification before accepting parcel delivery requests.

Verified Match Profiles

Senders review traveler profile verification status and past ratings before accepting a listing match.

Safe Handover Procedures

External package inspection at pickup helps verify item packaging and condition before transit.

Trust & Safety

Upholding trust through clear information, courteous communication, and active platform safety awareness.

Accurate Information

Users provide truthful parcel descriptions, dimensions, and addresses to ensure smooth coordination.

Respectful Communication

In-app messaging tools must be used exclusively for courteous, professional delivery coordination.

Responsible Behavior

Senders and travelers follow schedule agreements and handle packages with proper care during transit.

Safety Awareness

Handover guidelines encourage meeting in public, well-lit spaces for initial pickup and delivery.

Reporting Concerns

Built-in reporting tools allow users to flag policy violations or suspicious behavior directly to support.

Community Responsibility

Every platform member plays a key role in maintaining a safe, respectful marketplace environment.

Payment Escrow

Sender payments are held safely in escrow during transit and released to travelers upon QR code delivery confirmation.

Rating & Review System

Transparent feedback after every delivery maintains community reputation standards.

Dedicated Support Assistance

Our support team in Pune, Maharashtra assists users with inquiries and dispute reviews.
